# Metrora brand policy

Metrora is open-source software. Its source licence and its product identity serve different purposes.

## Brand identifiers

The following identify the official project and its publisher:

- the **Metrora** name;
- the **Signal Grid** mark, lockup, and application icon;
- the **Vensent** publisher name when used with Metrora.

No registered-trade-mark claim is made by this policy. Do not add the `®` symbol unless a relevant registration has been completed and verified.

## Permitted use

You may:

- refer truthfully to Metrora and link to the official project;
- use the canonical assets in screenshots, reviews, documentation, tutorials, and compatibility descriptions;
- redistribute an unmodified official Metrora build with its notices intact;
- state accurately that software is derived from, compatible with, or built using Metrora;
- preserve the Metrora name and assets where technically required to maintain attribution or compatibility.

## Uses requiring a distinct identity

A modified distribution, fork, hosted service, or commercial offering must use a clearly distinct product name and visual identity unless written permission has been granted.

It must not:

- imply that it is an official Metrora release;
- imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement, or publication by Vensent;
- reuse the Signal Grid app icon as its primary application identity;
- use confusingly similar branding that obscures who produced the software;
- remove upstream or Metrora provenance notices.

Accurate nominative references such as “based on Metrora” remain allowed when they are clear and non-misleading.

## Open-source licence boundary

The MIT licence grants broad rights in the software and documentation. It does not grant a right to impersonate the official project or misrepresent the origin of a distribution.

Canonical brand assets and usage rules are documented in [`assets/brand`](assets/brand/README.md).
