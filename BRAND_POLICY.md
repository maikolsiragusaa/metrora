# Metrora brand policy

Metrora is open-source software. Its source licence and official product identity serve different purposes.

## Official identifiers

Metrora™, Signal Grid™ and Vensent™ identify the official Metrora project, visual identity and publisher.

## Permitted use

You may:

- refer truthfully to Metrora and link to the official project;
- use canonical assets in screenshots, reviews, documentation, tutorials and compatibility descriptions;
- redistribute an unmodified official Metrora build with its notices intact;
- state accurately that software is derived from, compatible with or built using Metrora;
- preserve Metrora identifiers where technically required for attribution or compatibility.

## Distinct identity for modified offerings

A modified distribution, derivative product, hosted service or commercial offering must use a clearly distinct product name and visual identity unless written permission has been granted.

It must not:

- imply that it is an official Metrora release;
- imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement or publication by Vensent;
- reuse the Signal Grid application icon as its primary product identity;
- use confusingly similar branding that obscures who produced the offering;
- remove required upstream or Metrora notices.

Accurate nominative references such as “based on Metrora” remain allowed when clear and non-misleading.

## Source licence boundary

The MIT licence grants broad rights in the software and documentation. It does not grant a right to impersonate the official project or misrepresent the origin of a distribution.

Canonical assets and usage rules are documented in [`assets/brand`](assets/brand/README.md).
